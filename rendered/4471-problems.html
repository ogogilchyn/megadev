The counts come from the reports in reports-4471.json, one report counted once. The export has repeated rows from overlapping pages; they are removed first.

1. Parser reads day-first dates month first (cause: parser)

Day-first tenants get their dates read month first. "Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February." This is most of the ticket, and the one the queued fix is meant for.

Some reports under this cause do not fit a month-day swap: a date one day off ("Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May."), a meeting that landed on two days for two time zones, and one invite that arrived before the booking existed. The desk files them here by tenant, as causes.ts does. They need their own look before the fix is called complete.

2. Booking-v3 form hint is wrong for day-first tenants (cause: hint)

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants whose people write the day first (copper.studio, alder.physio, pale.harbour) type what the hint asks, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The parser fix does not touch this: the form has its own hint.

Do not close #4471 on the strength of the parser fix. It fixes cause 1 only, and cause 2 still stands.
