The counts above come from the reports in reports-4471.json, one report counted once. The export has repeated rows from overlapping pages; they are removed first.

1. Day and month swapped by the shared parser

Day-first tenants get their dates read month first. "Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February." This is the bulk of the ticket and the one the queued fix is meant for. Reports from every day-first tenant land here, through the app, email and forms, except the form case below.

2. Booking-v3 form hint is wrong for day-first tenants

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants whose people write the day first (copper.studio, alder.physio, pale.harbour) type what the hint asks, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The parser fix does not touch this: the form has its own hint. The desk's causes.ts already calls this a separate cause, hint.

3. Dates one day off, not swapped

The date moves by one day, which a swap cannot produce. Examples: "Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May." and "Every booking in June is one day off. Only June." One report from Lisbon and Toronto describes the same meeting landing on two days, which suggests a time zone issue. Cause not yet known.

4. Invite arrives before the booking exists

"The invite arrived before I made the booking. I would like to know how." One report, not explained by any of the above. Needs a look at the invite job's timestamps before it can be called a bug or noise.

What this changes

desk-notes.json lists five reports as one-day moves. Four are in problem 3. The fifth (R-41236) is problem 4.

Do not close #4471 on the strength of the parser fix. It fixes problem 1 only.
